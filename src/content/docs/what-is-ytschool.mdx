YTSchool turns the best of YouTube into structured learning. Instead of endless autoplay, you get Learning Goals, Paths, and optional live cohorts — so every minute of watching moves you forward.

## Why it exists

Great teachers are already on YouTube. They’re just hard to find in order. YTSchool curates and sequences that content so learners can progress with intent.

## What you get

- **Viewer** — explore curated videos and start learning for free.
- **Mastery** — unlock the full toolkit for long-term professional growth.

When the app launches, you sign in with Google (Gmail). That’s the only authentication method we support.
