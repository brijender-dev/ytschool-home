YTSchool authenticates with **Google only**. That keeps accounts simple and secure, and it matches how we invite people from the waitlist.

## What to use

Use a **Gmail** address (`@gmail.com` or `@googlemail.com`) when you:

1. Join the waitlist on this site
2. Sign in to the app after launch

## Why Gmail-only

Google sign-in is the single identity we support in the product. Collecting the same address on the waitlist means your invite maps cleanly to your account when the app opens.

## After launch

Open the app, choose **Continue with Google**, and pick the Gmail you registered with. Paid plans (Mastery) are managed inside the app after you sign in.
