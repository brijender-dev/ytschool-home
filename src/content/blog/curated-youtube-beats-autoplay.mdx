YouTube has the best teachers on earth — and the worst discovery model for serious learners.

YTSchool exists to fix that gap. Instead of letting the algorithm decide what you watch next, we curate paths, attach practice, and keep your notes tied to the exact second that mattered.

## Start with a goal

Pick an outcome (interview prep, a new stack, a design craft). Then follow a hand-picked sequence rather than a rabbit hole.

## Keep context

Timestamped notes, audio overviews, and quick bytes mean you can leave and return without losing the thread.
