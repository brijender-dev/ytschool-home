Motivation fades. Rhythm survives.

Here is a simple weekly pattern that works well with YTSchool:

1. **Monday** — choose one Learning Goal topic.
2. **Commute days** — pin the audio overview to My Podcasts.
3. **Weekend** — revise with flashcards and timestamped notes.
4. **Optional** — join a live cohort session for accountability.

Small, repeated reps beat binge weekends. Start free on the [Viewer plan](/pricing), then upgrade to Mastery when the habit earns it.
